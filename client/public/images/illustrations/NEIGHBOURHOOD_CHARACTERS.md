# TaskGenie: neighbourhood characters

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. Original artwork informed by the object characters and ink texture on [Fly.io](https://fly.io/), without reusing their artwork. The existing homepage and earlier illustration assets are preserved.

Three transparent WebP exports (960 x 640), with explicit dimensions, decorative alt text and lazy loading through ServiceIllustration. PNG originals remain in the Codex generated-images folder. No new runtime dependencies or image-host requests.

Placements: neighborhood-crew in the provider introduction; quiet-checkout and paper-trail in the recovery empty states.

## Final prompts

### neighborhood-crew.webp

Use case: illustration-story. Create an original decorative spot illustration for TaskGenie, a local home-services marketplace. Art direction: witty independent editorial comic, confident slightly imperfect deep-plum ink contour, dry pencil hatching and subtle screen-print grain, simple flat shapes, an imaginative little object-character with tiny dot eyes, expressive elongated limbs and pleasantly awkward proportions. Limited palette: lavender #bca5ef, deep plum #281950, butter-yellow #f6d673, pale mint #b9dfce, warm ivory, tiny coral accents. Clever and warm, sophisticated rather than childish. Transparent background with true alpha, isolated composition fully in frame, no rectangular background, no glow, no gradients, no text or numerals, no brand marks, no watermark, no UI cards. Definitely not anime, not Ghibli, not watercolor/gouache people, not soft fantasy scenery, not glossy 3D or generic corporate vector illustration. Landscape 3:2; compact scene with generous transparent margins, readable at 220px. Primary scene: a cheerful sturdy lavender toolbox with little legs and long gloved arms, carrying a rolled-up blank work order under one arm; a tall mustard-yellow adjustable-wrench companion and a small mint paint-roller companion walk alongside it toward a tiny simple coral-roofed house. A single little plant by the doorstep. They look like a small local crew heading out to help, each tool mechanically recognizable. Grounded playful composition, no floating objects.
